Qadam Rasul Mosque

Description

The Qadam Rasul is a beautifully proportioned square building featuring three arched entrances supported by thick stone pillars. Inside rests a small carved stone pedestal placed beneath the central dome, which is the focal point of the shrine.

History

Constructed in 1530 by Sultan Nusrat Shah, this revered monument was built to house a stone bearing the footprint (Qadam) of the Prophet Muhammad (Rasul). The relic was originally brought from Arabia by a devout merchant and holds immense religious significance.

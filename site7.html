Tantipara Mosque

Description

Renowned for possessing some of the finest terracotta floral decorations in India, the Tantipara Mosque is a rectangular brick structure. Even though its roof collapsed long ago, the beautifully ornate walls and mihrabs (prayer niches) make it a masterpiece of Bengal architecture.

History

Constructed in 1480 by Mirshad Khan, the mosque's name "Tantipara" translates to the "quarter of the weavers." It was built to serve the prosperous weaving community that once thrived in this specific district of the ancient capital of Gour.

Dakhil Darwaza

Description

The Dakhil Darwaza, also known as the Salami Darwaza, is an imposing gateway built of small red terracotta bricks. It features magnificent archways, guard rooms on both sides, and beautiful terracotta embellishments that showcase the pinnacle of brickwork in Bengal.

History

Built in the 15th century, it served as the main grand entrance to the citadel of Gour. Cannons used to be fired from here to welcome royal guests or celebrate victories, which is why it earned the nickname 'Salami Darwaza' (Gateway of Salutes).

Lottan Mosque

Description

The Lottan Mosque is famous for its surviving glazed colored tiles in shades of yellow, blue, green, and white. Though much of the vibrant exterior has faded over centuries, the intricate brickwork and octagonal corner towers remain a breathtaking sight.

History

Built around 1475, local legend claims it was commissioned by a royal dancing girl named Lottan. However, historical records suggest it was more likely constructed during the reign of Shamsuddin Yusuf Shah. It is one of the finest surviving examples of polychrome brickwork in Bengal.

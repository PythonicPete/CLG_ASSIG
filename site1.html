Adina Mosque

Description

The Adina Mosque, located in Pandua, is one of the most magnificent examples of medieval architecture in Bengal. At the time of its completion, it was the largest mosque in the Indian subcontinent, featuring a massive open courtyard surrounded by hundreds of domes and intricate arches.

History

It was built in 1373 by Sikandar Shah, the second Sultan of the Ilyas Shahi dynasty. The grand scale of the mosque was intended to showcase the Sultanate's immense power following their victories over the Delhi Sultanate. The mosque uniquely incorporates elements of pre-Islamic architecture in its stonework.

Eklakhi Mausoleum

Description

Located in Pandua, this is a massive square brick building topped with a single, giant dome. The structure is famous for being the earliest surviving square brick tomb in Bengal. Its thick walls feature intricate terracotta carvings of flowers and geometric patterns.

History

Built in the early 15th century, it is the tomb of Sultan Jalaluddin Muhammad Shah, the converted son of the Hindu king Raja Ganesha. The name "Eklakhi" comes from the historical record that its construction cost exactly one lakh (100,000) silver tanka coins.

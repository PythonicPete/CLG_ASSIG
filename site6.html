Lukochuri Gate

Description

Unlike the traditional terracotta structures of Gour, the Lukochuri Gate is built in the later Mughal architectural style. It is a massive three-story structure covered in smooth stucco plaster, featuring a large central archway flanked by smaller rooms.

History

Built around 1655 by Shah Shuja (brother of Emperor Aurangzeb), the name "Lukochuri" translates to "Hide and Seek." Legend states that the princes and their royal entourages used the numerous chambers and corridors of this gate to play hide-and-seek games.

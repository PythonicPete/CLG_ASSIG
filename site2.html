Firoz Minar

Description

Standing tall amidst the ruins of Gour, the Firoz Minar is a five-story tower measuring 26 meters in height. The first three stories are dodecagonal (12-sided), while the top two are circular. It closely resembles the Qutb Minar of Delhi in its architectural ambition.

History

The minar was constructed by Sultan Saifuddin Firuz Shah between 1485 and 1489 to commemorate his military victories. It served not only as a monument of triumph but also possibly as a muazzin's tower for a nearby mosque that no longer exists.

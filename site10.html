Qutb Shahi Mosque

Description

Also known locally as the Chhoti Sona Mosque (Small Golden Mosque) of Pandua, this rectangular stone and brick mosque features an open courtyard and five arched entryways. Its stone face gives it a rugged, durable look compared to the purely terracotta sites.

History

Constructed in 1582 by Makhdum Shaikh in honor of the revered saint Nur Qutb-ul-Alam. The saint was highly influential in the medieval politics of Bengal. The mosque served as a place of worship for the thousands of pilgrims visiting the saint's nearby shrine.

Chika Mosque

Description

A heavily fortified, single-domed square building, the Chika Mosque almost looks like a prison or a treasury from the outside due to its thick, massive walls. However, the interior contains beautiful traces of Hindu temple-style stonework repurposed in its arches.

History

Built by Sultan Mahmud Shah in 1450. Although it looks like a mosque, historians debate whether it was actually used as an administrative building, treasury, or prison. It got the name "Chika" (meaning bats) because it was abandoned for centuries and became a massive shelter for local bat colonies.

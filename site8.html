Baroduari Mosque (Bara Sona)

Description

Also known as the "Great Golden Mosque," the Baroduari Mosque is the largest surviving monument in Gour. Despite its name meaning "Twelve Doors," the massive stone structure actually features an eleven-arched corridor at its entrance.

History

It was built in 1526 by Sultan Nasrat Shah. The name "Bara Sona" (Great Golden) comes from the fact that its forty-four domes were originally gilded with gold. Though the gold is long gone and the roof has collapsed, the stone pillars and massive archways remain breathtaking.
